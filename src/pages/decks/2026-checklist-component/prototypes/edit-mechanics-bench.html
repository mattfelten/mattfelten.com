What each action writes

Click into an item and type. Autosave runs at 4s idle with a 15s ceiling.

Why there is no Save button in this round. A button is a promise that nothing is written until you press it. Inline editing makes the opposite promise: the text is the document, and leaving is the commit. Keeping both would be lying about one of them, so this round is autosave throughout and the only real question is how loudly it reports.

Watch the first line of the top item. Turn on Naive box model, click into it, and the whole paragraph shifts down and right by the padding. That jump is the reason inline editing usually feels cheap, and it is entirely avoidable: paint the state with background and box-shadow, both of which sit outside layout.
