Why one level and not many. One level keeps every problem worth having: the index stops being flat, dragging has to carry two things at once, and a parent's checkbox has to mean something. Going deeper adds recursion and a much heavier keyboard contract without adding a new question. A checklist that needs grandchildren is a document, and should be one.

The index is now scoped to a parent. A position only means anything next to its siblings, so moving between levels is not the same operation as moving within one: the row leaves one sequence and joins another. Watch the log with Integer switched on to see how much worse that gets when positions are not fractional.
